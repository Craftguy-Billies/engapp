Top Recommendations
1. WordNet (MIT License) - Best for Relationships
License: MIT - Free commercial use, no attribution required
Content: 155,000 words with synonyms, antonyms, hypernyms
Pros: Free, comprehensive, semantic relationships
Cons: No CEFR levels, no frequency data
Use for: Word relationships (synonyms, antonyms)
2. CEFR-J (CC BY-SA 4.0) - Best for Levels
License: CC BY-SA 4.0 - Free commercial use, must attribute
Content: 6,000 words with CEFR levels (A1-C2)
Pros: Official CEFR categorization
Cons: Limited word count, requires attribution
Use for: CEFR level tagging
3. EFL Lexicon (CC BY 4.0) - Best for Frequency
License: CC BY 4.0 - Free commercial use, must attribute
Content: 5,000 words with frequency, POS, difficulty
Pros: Research-backed frequency data
Cons: Limited word count, requires attribution
Use for: Frequency ranking, difficulty levels
4. Wiktionary (CC BY-SA 3.0) - Best for Definitions
License: CC BY-SA 3.0 - Free commercial use, must attribute
Content: 500,000+ words with definitions, translations
Pros: Massive, multilingual
Cons: Quality varies, requires attribution
Use for: Definitions, translations
